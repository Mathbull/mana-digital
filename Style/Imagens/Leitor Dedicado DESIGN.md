## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, cyber-logistics gaming environment engineered specifically for corporate training in racial equality, Brazilian anti-racism legal compliance (such as Lei 7.716/1989 and the Estatuto da Igualdade Racial), and inclusive corporate governance. The system balances gaming progression dynamics with corporate operational precision.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Tone:** Authoritative, forward-looking, empowering, and strictly compliant. It eliminates frivolous novelty in favor of enterprise gamification: telemetry dashboards, metric trackers, patent progression badges, and immersive legal case study simulations.
- **Visual Stance:** Cyber-logistics dark mode. It leverages deep space blues, resonant midnight navy substrates, and laser-precise electric indigo highlights, reinforced by metallic tier signifiers (Bronze, Prata, Ouro, Diamante).
- **Target Experience:** Employees and logistics leaders experience racial literacy and compliance training not as a passive checklist, but as an operational qualification matrix driven by verified XP, level advancements, and actionable workplace scenarios.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-high-contrast dark environment built around deep midnight foundations and luminescent tactical accents.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#4338CA` - Electric Indigo):** Anchors operational focus. Used for primary state actions, XP milestones, interactive keyframes, and master telemetry points.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Cyber Blue):** Used for forward progression indicators, live simulation inputs, active radar outlines, and interactive links.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Vibrant Azure):** Highlights active micro-interactions, floating badges, and high-priority legal citations.
- **Neutral Substrates (`#0B0F19` down to `#0F172A`):** The structural canvas, reminiscent of flight management systems and enterprise network terminals.

### Progression Tiers (Patentes)
- **Bronze (`#CD7F32` / `#D97706`):** Foundations, introductory anti-bias credentials, baseline legal modules.
- **Prata (`#94A3B8` / `#CBD5E1`):** Operational compliance, intermediate case resolution, cross-functional logistics synergy.
- **Ouro (`#F59E0B` / `#FCD34D`):** Strategic leadership, advanced conflict mitigation, equitable workforce management.
- **Diamante (`#38BDF8` / `#E0F2FE`):** Executive mastery, institutional governance, systemic policy transformation.

### Semantics & Status
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Correct compliance choices, verified XP gain.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Procedural legal risk, sensitive workplace scenario flagged.
- **Danger (`#EF4444`):** Direct legal non-compliance, discriminatory incident violation.
- **Info (`#06B6D4`):** Legal references (Lei 7.716, CF/88 Art. 5º, Jurisprudência).

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising hierarchy of operational clarity. 

- **Display & Headings (`Sora`):** Imparts geometric weight and cybernetic sophistication. Headings above 24px apply negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) to eliminate loose horizontal rhythms, preserving high authority across patent rank announcements, scenario titles, and performance metrics.
- **Body & Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly engineered grotesk with open apertures, optimal for sustained readability during legal analysis, incident reviews, and dense compliance prompts on dark screens.
- **Tactical Data & Statutory Citations (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to law references (e.g., `LEI 12.288/2010 - ART. 39`), telemetry values, latency stats, and direct XP scores to emphasize data-backed accountability.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture models high-density supply-chain command centers. It applies a rigid 8-point vertical grid alongside a 12-column adaptive layout.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, max-width 1440px with balanced margins. Left-hand HUD navigation stays pinned, module canvas occupies central 8 columns, and real-time XP/Patent status anchors the right 3 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, collapsible secondary diagnostic panel.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, bottom dock controls for XP progress and operational action triggers.

### Rhythm & Density
Whitespace is structured rather than expansive. Spacing separates cognitive clusters: modules group relevant data tightly (`space-sm` to `space-base`), while discrete decision gates and legal branch nodes use wider gaps (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) to prevent input fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through luminous surface layering and high-precision tactical borders rather than diffuse, heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Substrate (Level 0 - Ground):** `#070A11` deep void background. Serves as base for the entire canvas.
2. **Deck Surface (Level 1):** `#0F172A` with a 1px border styled as `rgba(67, 56, 202, 0.25)`. Houses background metric tables, logs, and inactive missions.
3. **Interactive Panels (Level 2):** `#1E293B` layered over Deck, rimmed with a 1px border of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` and a subtle blue ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(67, 56, 202, 0.15)`). Houses active scenarios, legal quizzes, and training branch nodes.
4. **Active Flight Overlays & Dialogs (Level 3):** `#1E1B4B` (deep indigo tint) bounded by `1px solid #38BDF8` with dynamic dual-layered elevation: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`. Houses patent promotions, violation alerts, and emergency drill actions.

### Optical Detailing
- **Ghost Outlines:** Every card or panel utilizes a 1px inner or perimeter border with low-opacity cybernetic hues (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `rgba(67, 56, 202, 0.4)`) to maintain component edges on deep dark canvases.
- **Surface Sheen:** High-rank elements feature a 1px top highlight gradient running from transparent to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)` to transparent, replicating aerospace HUD glass.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, technical shape language. High roundedness is discarded to avoid playful casualness; geometry remains sharp, disciplined, and purposeful.

### Corner Radius System (`roundedness: 1` / Soft)
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Clean, engineered, and tactical.
- **Card Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Delivers high structural integrity without breaking dark-mode layout grids.
- **Framed Displays & XP Chambers (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`). Reserved for full-frame module cards and progression vaults.
- **Tactical Pills (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for numerical XP indicators, status diodes, and patent chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber-Action:** Background in `#4338CA`, text in `#FFFFFF`, font `Sora` 14px Weight 600. Border: `1px solid #6366F1`. Glow on hover: `0 0 14px rgba(67, 56, 202, 0.6)`. Active state shifts to `#3730A3`.
- **Secondary HUD Action:** Transparent background with `#06B6D4` text and `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`. Hover transitions surface to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)` with subtle glow.
- **Destructive/Violation Action:** Deep crimson `#7F1D1D` background with `#FCA5A5` text and red beacon glow. Used for flagging systemic racial harassment or reporting severe infractions.

### XP Progress Bars & Telemetry Gauges
- **Track Base:** Dark recessed trough `#0B0F17` with inset shadow and 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Progress Fill:** High-voltage horizontal gradient from `#4338CA` to `#06B6D4`. Finished modules pulse with `#38BDF8` terminal lights.
- **Segmented Milestone Pips:** Tick marks representing scenario decision points, highlighted in accordance with user performance.

### Patent Badges (Rank System)
- **Structure:** Hexagonal or chamfered metallic badges carrying the rank insignia, patent level, and anti-racism legal mastery validation.
- **Bronze (Bronze):** Brushed bronze metallic finish (`#D97706` base) with warm amber backlight.
- **Prata (Silver):** Polished steel finish (`#94A3B8`) with bright cold-white edge highlights.
- **Ouro (Gold):** Rich gold luster (`#F59E0B`) with radiant aureate rim.
- **Diamante (Diamond):** Prismatic cyber-refraction using layered electric cyan `#38BDF8` and deep indigo glow `#4338CA`.

### Interactive Case Study Cards (Scenario Decision Nodes)
- **Card Substrate:** Layered container in `#111827` featuring a 1px border in `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.
- **Legal Annotation Strip:** Monospaced banner anchoring the top right: `LEI 7.716/89 - INJÚRIA RACIAL E DISCRIMINAÇÃO`.
- **Option Selectors:** Multiple-choice operational responses styled as radio tiles with 4px radius. Selection triggers cyan outline illumination and instant analytical feedback.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0B0F17`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #334155`. On focus: border shifts to `#06B6D4` accompanied by inner glow `0 0 8px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom 16px geometric squares/circles with 1px border. Selected state filled with `#4338CA` containing crisp white vectors.
- **Status Tags / Legal Chips:** Ultra-compact tags (11px, bold, uppercase) embedded in deep containers with 20% opacity borders matching the respective semantic state (e.g., Red for non-compliance risk, Blue for established jurisprudence).
